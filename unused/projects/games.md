## [Horizon](https://horizon.projects.filipepcampos.com)

This game was developed in 2018 and it's pretty much the first large scale game I've ever created. This simple 2D platformer takes the player through multiple levels facing a variety of enemies along the way (actually, there's just 2 enemy types, but let's pretend there's a lot of variety).

**Controls:**
```
WASD / Arrows - Movement
X / K - Attack
Space - Jump
Esc / P - Pause
```
